The Venue & Property

Forty-two acres where the Blue Ridge foothills meet running water — a pavilion, a barn, a waterfall, and room for 200 of your favorite people.

The Heart of the Property

A 40×60 open-air pavilion, lit from beneath

Seated between two streams that merge into a small river, the pavilion is the centerpiece of every Stillwater event. Open on all sides to the breeze and the woods, it holds up to 200 guests comfortably — with lighting built into the structure for the moments after the sun drops behind the ridge.

Daylight ceremony or evening reception, rain or shine: the covered floor keeps the celebration going, and the sound of the water never stops playing in the background.

  • Fits up to 200 guests with dance floor and tables
  • Integrated lighting beneath the roof
  • Open-air, covered setting beside two streams
  • Nearby waterfall for portraits and quiet moments
For Your Caterer

The catering barn

A unique and gorgeous location for your caterer to work — and for your guests to wander into. The barn gives your catering team covered, comfortable space to plate and prep, while staying close enough to the pavilion that service never breaks rhythm.

Outside catering is welcome, and the property includes two BBQs for grilling on-site.

  • Covered prep and serving space for your caterer
  • Outside catering welcome
  • Two BBQs included with the property
  • A warm, character-filled gathering spot
Guest Comfort

The details that make guests feel cared for

An outdoor venue shouldn't mean roughing it. Every comfort detail is built in.

AC Bathrooms & Changing Rooms

Separate air-conditioned bathrooms and changing rooms for men and women — a cool, calm place to get ready or freshen up mid-evening.

Patio Heaters & BBQs

Four patio heaters keep the pavilion comfortable into the evening; two BBQs support your caterer or your own cookout.

Accessible & Easy Parking

ADA/ACA accessible grounds, on-site private parking for 200+ guests, and a private road that keeps the venue exclusive to your party.

Water & Woods

Two streams, one waterfall, and all the stillness you can hold.

The property's streams meet beside the pavilion and continue as a small river through the meadow. There is a waterfall within an easy walk — the backdrop for portraits, first looks, and the moments you'll want to relive.

Walking the property is the only way to understand it.
