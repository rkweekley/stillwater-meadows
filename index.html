Welcome to Stillwater Meadows

A place that already feels like home — before the vows are even said.

Found in the foothills of the Blue Ridge Mountains is a perfect outdoor venue for your special day.

Stillwater Meadows sits at the end of a private road on 42 acres of field, forest and moving water. Two streams run through the property and merge into a small river beside the pavilion — with a nearby waterfall and all the serenity you could hope to find before an event begins.

From a 40×60 open-air pavilion lit from beneath to a catering barn that feels like it grew out of the hillside, every corner was imagined for gathering: weddings, reunions, celebrations, and quiet moments in between.
